## JAHMERE WEBB: THE BRIDGE BUILDER

### The Case Facts
- Entered system at 21 with documented mental age of 15
- 25-year sentence vs typical 5-7 years (disparity due to firearm enhancement)
- 11 years served (44% of sentence under Florida's 85% rule)
- Developmental delays documented in 2013 psychological evaluation
- Anti-Murder Act (Florida Statute 948.06) limits judicial discretion

### The Transformation Evidence
- 13 character witness testimonials documenting complete transformation
- Unanimous evaluation: "will not recidivate"
- Co-founded Bridge Project while incarcerated
- Developed AI-powered mentorship concept based on lived experience
- Became authentic leader and mentor to other incarcerated individuals

### The Economic Case
- Current incarceration cost: $28,800/year × 14 remaining years = $403,200
- Treatment alternative cost: ~$109,200 over 14 years
- Direct savings: $294,000
- Recidivism rate: 37.6% for intellectually disabled vs 21.2% general population
- Treatment reduces recidivism to <15%

### The Bridge Project Vision
- JAHmere as authentic mentor with lived experience
- Algorithm based on his understanding of what creates real transformation
- Community problem-solving approach developed from his incarceration insights
- Proof that those who've walked the path can best guide others
